GTA VI's Japanese version tones down nudity and blood

Players in Japan found swimsuits in the shower, less gore and no way to turn it back up. Rockstar did not announce the changes ahead of launch.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 21, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI players in Japan are getting a noticeably tamer game than the rest of the world, and many only found out once they started playing.

What's different

According to Kotaku and VGC, which both cite footage and screenshots shared by Japanese players, nudity has been removed or covered throughout. Jason and Lucia wear swimwear in shower scenes, sex scenes and beach areas have been changed, and some scenes are reportedly cut altogether.

Violence is also dialed back. Japanese outlet GameSpark reports that blood from injured and dead characters is reduced, disappears faster, and that decapitations have been removed. Elsewhere, players can lower gore and switch off dismemberment in the settings, but the Japanese version offers no option to raise it. VGC says the changes appear to be cosmetic, and the missions themselves play out the same way.

Warning signs before launch

Fans had suspected regional changes for weeks. Kotaku says the Japanese download was more than 1GB smaller than other regions, and the game's CERO rating in Japan left out the sexual content descriptors listed by rating boards elsewhere. Rockstar did not confirm any changes before release. Australian and New Zealand downloads are also slightly smaller, VGC notes, but there is no evidence yet that those versions are edited.

Kotaku also reports that the Japanese release carries only English voice acting with English and Japanese subtitles, while other regions offer more than a dozen languages but no Japanese text. Censoring Japanese releases is not new for the series, which has made similar edits in past entries.
