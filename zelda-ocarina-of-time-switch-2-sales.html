Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sells 5.2 million in four days, a Switch 2 record

Nintendo's rebuilt classic is now the fastest-selling game on Switch 2.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 13, 2026

Nintendo says The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 sold more than 5.2 million copies worldwide in its first four days. That makes it the fastest-selling game on the console so far.

The remake launched on November 5 as a major modern overhaul of the 1998 Nintendo 64 classic, the same game whose 99 Metacritic score GTA VI tied this week. It's also one of six Game of the Year nominees at The Game Awards 2026.

Also new on Switch 2

Nintendo had a busy week beyond Zelda. Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy came out on November 12, and the eShop now lets you gift digital games to members of your family group. The second part of Pokémon Pokopia's paid expansion, Dazzling Decorations, arrives December 9.
