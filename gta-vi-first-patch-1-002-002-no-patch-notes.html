GTA VI gets its first post-launch patch, with no patch notes

Update 1.002.002 is rolling out in stages on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar hasn't said what it changes.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 23, 2026

Rockstar has started rolling out the first Grand Theft Auto VI update since the game launched on November 19. Version 1.002.002 on PS5, listed as 1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S, began reaching players on November 23.

According to Push Square and Tbreak, the download is about 604MB on PS5 and 1.6GB on Xbox Series X|S. It is the third patch in roughly ten days, following two pre-launch updates on November 13 and November 16.

A staggered release

The update is not arriving for everyone at once. Rockstar dataminer Tex2 reported that it would reach a third of PS5 owners first, two thirds after about three hours and everyone within six hours. Push Square says the staging appears designed to avoid overloading servers. Players who don't see it yet can check for updates manually from the game's options menu on console.

What it fixes

Rockstar has not published patch notes, and neither of the earlier updates came with any. That leaves players guessing. Some reports describe the game feeling smoother after the update, but there is no confirmation of performance changes, and Tbreak cautions that this may be a placebo effect. The game's 30fps target on consoles, including PS5 Pro, is unchanged as far as anyone knows.

Players have reported a handful of launch bugs, including a PS5 error code and an Xbox glitch that runs gameplay at double speed. It is not known whether this patch addresses either.
