God of War Sons of Sparta comes to PS4 with two free new modes

The 2D Kratos prequel is no longer PS5-only. A free update on both consoles adds a speedrun mode and a brutal one-hit mode.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 13, 2026

God of War Sons of Sparta, the 2D pixel-art spin-off that Sony shadow-dropped on PS5 in February for the series' 20th anniversary, came to PlayStation 4 on Monday, November 16.

The game follows Kratos as a 13-year-old Spartan soldier, long before his war on the gods. It was developed by Mega Cat Studios with Santa Monica Studio. A PS4 version had been rumored after dataminers spotted a PS4 listing earlier in November, but the new content was a surprise.

Two new ways to play

A free update for both PS4 and PS5 adds two modes. You choose one when you start a new game in a save slot, and you can't switch later.

  • Hermes Mode: a speedrun-focused mode that unlocks after you finish the game.
  • Erebus Mode: a punishing challenge where both you and your enemies die in a single hit.

What's next for Kratos

The main series is further off than planned. Santa Monica Studio recently delayed God of War Laufey from February to May 20, 2027.
