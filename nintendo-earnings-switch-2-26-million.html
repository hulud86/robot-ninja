Switch 2 reaches 26.35 million sold as Nintendo holds its 16.5 million forecast

Hardware sales are down from launch year, but software is up and Nintendo is sticking to its full-year target despite rising chip costs.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 5, 2026

Nintendo's results for April to September show Switch 2 settling into its second year. The console sold 2.67 million units in the July–September quarter, bringing its lifetime total to 26.35 million.

First-half hardware sales came to 6.49 million, down 37.4% from a year earlier. That comparison is against the console's launch period, when demand was at its peak. Software is moving the other way: Switch 2 games sold 24.49 million units in the half, up 18.8%.

Holding the line

Nintendo kept its full-year forecast of 16.5 million Switch 2 consoles for the year ending March 2027. That forecast already allows for around $633 million in extra costs from rising component prices, with memory chips the main pressure.

The original Switch is still selling too. It added 1.27 million units in the half, for a lifetime total of 157.20 million.

The quarter ended before the November 5 launch of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, which has since become the fastest-selling Switch 2 game. Its effect will show up in next quarter's numbers.
