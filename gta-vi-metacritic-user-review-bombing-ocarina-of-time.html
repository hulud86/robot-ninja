GTA VI's Metacritic user score sinks to 5.6 as fans start review-bombing Zelda

Thousands of zero scores have hit GTA VI's user page, and some GTA fans have retaliated against Ocarina of Time, which held Metacritic's top user score.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 23, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI may be the best-reviewed game on Metacritic, but its user score tells a different story. According to VGC, the game's user rating had fallen to 5.6 out of 10 from more than 10,000 reviews as of November 23, against a critic score of 99.

Metacritic held back user reviews until 36 hours after launch, its usual practice, after removing fake reviews that had been posted before the game was out. Once the page opened, a large share of the scores were zeros.

What the low scores say

VGC reports that many of the zero-score reviews complain about the game's story, describing it as a relationship simulator built around keeping co-lead Lucia happy. Others use culture-war language to attack the game. VGC called the pattern of zero scores suspicious.

The retaliation

Some GTA fans have responded by targeting other games at the top of Metacritic's charts. The 2026 Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, which had the highest user score on the site, has received a wave of zero ratings over the past few days. Several reviewers say they are GTA fans who believe Zelda fans bombed GTA VI first.

The remake's user score was still 8.3 when VGC checked, propped up by tens of thousands of positive reviews posted after its launch. The original 1998 Ocarina of Time and SoulCalibur, third on the all-time list, have also picked up zero scores from GTA fans.

Why it matters

User scores on Metacritic tend to cluster at zero and ten, and coordinated campaigns have pushed games up and down the lists before. The swings say more about fan rivalries than about how good any of these games are. Critic scores for both GTA VI and Ocarina of Time are unaffected.
