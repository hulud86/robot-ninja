GTA VI reviews are in, and it just tied the highest Metacritic score ever

Rockstar's game opened at 99 on Metacritic when the review embargo lifted, matching Ocarina of Time's 1998 record. Here's what critics loved and what they didn't.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 18, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI's review embargo lifted early on Wednesday, November 18, and the game immediately settled at a 99 on Metacritic. That ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, which has sat at the top of the site's all-time chart since 1998.

The sample sizes aren't comparable. Ocarina's 99 comes from a couple of dozen reviews written before online criticism exploded, while GTA VI reached the same number across roughly 147 PS5 reviews, every one of them positive. On OpenCritic it sits at 98, making it the top-rated game on both aggregators. IGN and GameSpot were among the outlets awarding a perfect 10.

What critics flagged

The praise wasn't unconditional. The lowest score so far, an 80 from Australian outlet ScreenHub, called some stretches epic and others dull and restrictive. The Guardian's review liked the game but found the controls complicated and occasionally unfair. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier felt the story doesn't hit the emotional peaks of Red Dead Redemption 2.

The money side

Investors noticed. Shares in Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, climbed as much as 8.4% in New York trading on the news.

What happens next

Launch-day scores often drift as more reviews arrive, and player verdicts after release can look different from critics'. We'll keep an eye on both. GTA VI launches Thursday, November 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
