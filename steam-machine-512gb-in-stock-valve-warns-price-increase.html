Steam Machine 512GB is now in stock, but Valve warns prices may rise

Valve has cleared its reservation queue for the base model and closed the 2TB waitlist, while warning that soaring memory and storage costs could push prices up again.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 20, 2026

Getting hold of a Steam Machine just got easier, at least for the base model. In an update posted to its Steam hardware community group on November 19, Valve said it has fulfilled every reservation for the 512GB Steam Machine and still has stock left. The 512GB model is now simply available to buy on its Steam store page while supplies last, and it ships right away.

Valve had said in July that it expected to catch up on the reservation queue by the end of 2026. That has now happened for the 512GB version.

2TB waitlist closed

The 2TB model is a different story. Valve says it has sent purchase invitations to everyone holding a 2TB reservation, and people already on the waitlist keep their place. But it is no longer taking new signups.

Prices could climb

The bigger news for anyone still waiting is a warning about cost. Valve says it won't be able to build Steam Machines in the numbers it had planned, citing memory and storage costs that continue to increase at unprecedented levels. The company says it is still producing more units but doesn't yet know how many it can make or at what price, and it expects the situation to continue for at least a few months.

In practice, that means today's price may not last. If you were planning to buy a 512GB Steam Machine, the current stock is the safest bet.
