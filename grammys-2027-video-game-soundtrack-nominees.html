Ghost of Yotei, Marvel's Wolverine and 007 First Light lead Grammy video game nominees

Five scores are up for the Grammy for best video game soundtrack, while the team behind Unbeatable says its vocal-heavy music was a poor fit for the category.

By Robot Ninja staff, November 19, 2026

The Recording Academy has named its nominees for the video game soundtrack Grammy, and Sony's first-party studios are well represented.

The nominees

  • Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora – From the Ashes, by John Paesano
  • 007 First Light, by Joe Henson and Alexis Leon Smith
  • Ghost of Yotei, by Toma Otowa
  • Marvel's Wolverine, by David Fleming
  • Star Wars Zero Company, by Gordy Haab

The winner will be named at the 69th Grammy Awards on Sunday, February 7, 2027. As in past years, the game category is handed out at the premiere ceremony a few hours before the televised show. Haab is a previous winner, having shared the award for Star Wars Jedi: Survivor.

The vocals question

The nominations also sparked a debate about what kind of music the category rewards. RJ Lake, writer and director of rhythm game Unbeatable from D-Cell Games, said on Bluesky that the game's largely vocal soundtrack wasn't a good match for the award. After the studio submitted it for consideration, Lake said, the Grammys indicated that songs with vocals aren't what the category's voters are looking for.

According to Game Developer, that isn't a written rule, more an understanding of what the voting body expects. Lake acknowledged the game probably wouldn't have been nominated anyway. Still, the category has never nominated a soundtrack built mainly around vocal songs, which leaves a lot of game music out of the running.
